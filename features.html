Features of Secure E-Voting System

1. Blockchain Security

Ensures a tamper-proof, transparent, and immutable voting process.

2. Multi-Factor Authentication

Voter identity is verified through Aadhaar, PAN, and OTP authentication.

3. Remote Voting Access

Allows eligible voters to cast their vote from anywhere securely.

4. Biometric & Facial Recognition

Enhances security by using biometric and facial recognition login.

5. Instant Vote Confirmation

Voters receive immediate confirmation upon successfully casting their vote.

6. Transparency & Auditability

Election results are verifiable and cannot be altered post-voting.
