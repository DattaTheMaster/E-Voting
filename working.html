Home How it Works Get Started

Step 1: Registration

Users register using their Aadhaar, PAN, and OTP verification for secure authentication.

Step 2: Secure Login

Biometric or facial recognition ensures only eligible voters can access their accounts.

Step 3: Casting the Vote

Voters securely select their candidate, and the vote is encrypted and stored using blockchain technology.

Step 4: Verification & Transparency

Votes are securely verified and can be audited for transparency while maintaining voter anonymity.
